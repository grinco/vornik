Configuration assistant

Proposals →

Describe a change to a project's autonomy settings, swarm or workflows in plain language. The assistant edits a private, secret-sanitized copy of the configuration, validates the result with the daemon's own loaders, classifies its blast radius, has a separate judge model check it, and files a proposal for you to approve. Classes C, D and E never auto-apply; class E needs your explicit approval and shows the effective permission diff.
